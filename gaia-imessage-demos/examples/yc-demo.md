# Worked example: the raise

The accepted 2:42 cut. One founder, four weeks, one raise, one personal thread.

Use it as a shape, not a template to copy. Every scene exists because it proves something the previous scene could not.

## Why this spine

The claim: **it runs the parts of your life and your company that would otherwise wait for you.**

The raise gives the month a clock. The birthday gives it a heart. The passport and the paperwork prove it is not only a work tool. The flight refund and the cancelled subscription give it money.

| Dec 4 close | No work, one personal note | Adjacency |

## The ending

"No work today" is the claim. The mother's call is not work, so it does not contradict the line. If the line were "nothing on your plate," the call would be a contradiction. The distinction is the whole point.

## What to cut for 60 seconds

Keep Nov 2, Nov 14, and Dec 4. The morning brief, the refund, the close. Cut everything between. The claim survives.
