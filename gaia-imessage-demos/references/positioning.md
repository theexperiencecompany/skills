# Positioning

The demo is judged against five products that launched into the same slot. A YC partner has seen their demos. The script has to differ in a way the viewer can see, not in a way the narrator claims.

## The set

| Product | Who it is for | The impact it claims | What its demo shows | What it avoids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Folk | Consumers | "Keeps you on track" | Habits, streaks, briefs, price watching | Money, business risk |
| Instinct | Invite-only consumers | Errands disappear | Bookings, DMV, follow-ups, a cloud VM | Failure, liability, its own terms backlash |
| Muse | Everyone, free | Everyday tasks and long-term goals | Shopping, tickets, scheduling, a secure VM | A business number |
| Dots | ChatGPT Pro users | Work continues between conversations | Project ownership, Slack, approvals | A hard result |
| Duet | Professionals and small businesses | Judgment about what needs you | Brief, unpaid invoices, payroll, contracts | A real customer |

## The convergence

Every one of them demos the same shape. A clean task completes. None shows a task that stalls for three weeks, a decision that costs money, or a customer who does not come back.

That means the contractor-admin story is no longer differentiating. Duet's launch copy already lists invoices, payroll, and contracts. Telling it better does not help.

## The three positions still open

| Position | The impact | The proof the thread can show | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open and auditable | You can see and host what it did | A month ledger with receipts, and the one quote it refused | Reads as infrastructure |
| The one that says no | It protects your margin | It holds a quote that is wrong, or refuses a bad job | One beat, not a demo |
| Compounding | Two jobs become four | Same hands, double the work | Duet and Dots claim it |

Pick one before writing. Do not blend all three. The claim has to survive being said in one sentence.

## What not to do

Do not name a competitor in the video. State the principle, not the comparison. "We charge you, not the businesses we book" is a principle. "Unlike Instinct" is an attack.

Do not lead with "open source" as the story. It is a proof point, not a plot.

Do not claim memory as the differentiator. Every product in the set has memory.

## The application must match

The demo's protagonist and the application's one-liner have to tell the same story. If the application says "personal assistant for daily life" and the demo shows a founder running a company, a partner will ask which company this is.

Choose one:

- Consumer daily life, and the demo is a person, not a company.
- Founders and operators, and the application says so.
- Service businesses, and the application says so.

Change the line before you change the script. The demo is the evidence for the application, not a separate pitch.
